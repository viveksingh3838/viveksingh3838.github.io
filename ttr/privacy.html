Privacy Policy — Tiny Track Rivals

Effective date: 6 November 2026 · App version this applies to: 1.0.0 and later

Tiny Track Rivals ("the game", "the app") is an Android racing game made by Xure Solutions ("we", "us"), package name in.xuresolutions.tinytrackrivals. This policy explains what information the game and the services built into it handle, why, and the choices you have. It applies only to this app.

In short. The game has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own that stores anything about you. Your progress stays on your phone. The game itself collects no personal information. Ads and a few optional Google and Tenjin services can process technical data (such as your IP address and device identifiers) when they are switched on, as described below.

1. What stays on your phone

  • Your game save — progress, unlocked racers and karts, coins, settings and the racer name you type — is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone. We never upload it and we cannot see it.
  • Small settings files — for example the last configuration values received, how many races since the last ad, and which promotional picture was shown last — are also kept only on the phone.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes all of this. Settings ▸ Reset progress in the game erases your progress too.

2. What the game does not do

  • No account, no login, no social sign-in, no chat, no multiplayer with strangers.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location. The app asks for no such permission.
  • No in-app purchases and no payment information.
  • We do not sell your information.

3.1 House ads (our own promotions)

Between some races, the game may show a full-screen promotion for our game Snake Haul. The promotion is loaded over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) from our own server, haul.xuresolutions.in, and shown in a web view inside the game. Like any web request, this reveals your device's IP address and the web view's standard browser information (user agent) to our server, which may keep ordinary access logs for security and operation. The game sends no identifier and no game data with it. The promotion cannot access your files or location. Tapping it opens Snake Haul's page in the Google Play Store. Promotions are never shown during a race, nor during your first levels, and can be closed after a few seconds. With no internet connection, none are shown.

3.2 Google AdMob (when switched on)

When we turn ads on, the game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show rewarded ads (which you choose to watch, for a bonus) and occasional full-screen ads between races. Google may then collect and process: your IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device and app identifiers such as the app set ID and — where allowed — the Android advertising ID, information about your interactions with ads, and diagnostic data about the SDK. Google uses it to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and Settings ▸ Privacy & Ads lets you change your choice. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads.

The current version (1.0.0) carries no AdMob account configuration and does not include the permission to read the advertising ID, so AdMob cannot run in it. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites; Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3.3 Google Firebase Remote Config (when included)

Remote Config lets us change game settings (for example how often ads may appear, or event rewards) without an app update. When a version includes it, the app fetches settings from Google Firebase, which processes a Firebase installation ID (a random identifier for this installation of the app), the app version, the device's language and region setting and the time of the request. This is used only to deliver the right settings, not for advertising. Firebase Analytics is switched off in the app and collects nothing. The current version does not run Firebase. Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3.4 Tenjin (when switched on)

Tenjin is a measurement service that tells us which advertising campaign, if any, led to an install, and counts a few game events (such as a race finished or a level cleared, and ad impressions). When switched on, Tenjin may process device identifiers (the app set ID, Tenjin's own install ID and, where allowed, the advertising ID), your IP address, the Google Play install referrer, basic device information (model, Android version, language, app version) and those events. It is used for analytics and attribution. Tenjin is never switched on for an audience that is directed at children, and it respects your answer to the ad consent form where one was shown. It is off in the current version. Tenjin's privacy policy: tenjin.com/privacy.

4. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

Storing your save on your phone is needed to provide the game. Personalised advertising and attribution rely on your consent where the law requires it, collected through Google's consent form. Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention, security logs and delivering settings rely on our legitimate interest in running a free, ad-supported game safely.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers described above, when they are active, and only for the purposes described. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Security and retention

All network traffic from the game uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Your game save never leaves your phone. Access logs on our promotion server are kept only as long as needed for operation and security. Data processed by Google and Tenjin is kept according to their own policies linked above.

7. Children's privacy

Tiny Track Rivals is a cartoon racing game with cartoon-style vehicle combat. The current version is offered on Google Play to players aged 13 and over; we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game itself asks for no personal information from anyone — the racer name you type never leaves the phone.

If we ever offer the game to an audience that includes children under 13, we will follow Google Play's Families policy: advertising would be tagged as child-directed (contextual, non-personalised, rated for general audiences), the advertising ID would not be used, no consent form would be shown to children, and Tenjin would stay off.

If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad consent choice in Settings ▸ Privacy & Ads in the game (where a choice applies).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings.
  • Delete everything the game stores on your phone by uninstalling it or clearing its storage.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because we hold no account or profile about you, most requests are best handled with the services above directly; we will help with any request sent to us.
