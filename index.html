01 — Android app

Jaap

A devotional companion for daily mantra jaap, panchang and vrat — in ten Indian languages.

  • Tap or voice counting: the phone listens for the mantra and counts it, using on-device speech recognition.
  • Panchang worked out on the phone from astronomical formulas — tithi, nakshatra, sunrise, Rahu kaal, Ekadashi paaran.
  • Janam kundli with North and South Indian charts, Vimshottari dasha, kundli milan (36 gunas) and a vivah muhurat finder.
  • 146 vrat kathas, regional festival calendars, a home-screen widget and reminders. 180+ automated tests.

02 — Multiplayer game

Seep

Real-time online version of Seep (Sweep), the Indian fishing card game, for two players.

  • Server-authoritative: the game engine is pure, immutable functions; clients only send moves and get filtered state back.
  • Rooms and matchmaking over socket.io, with bots at four levels — from random play to a tuned heuristic strategy.
  • Every state change is logged as JSONL to train a neural-network bot.
  • Shipped to Android with Capacitor; deploys through GitHub Actions to EC2 with PM2.

03 — Mobile game

Tiny Track Rivals

A portrait arcade kart racer for Android, built in Unity 6.

  • Story campaign across eight worlds with rival duels and boss races.
  • Combat powers (rockets, magnet, side-step), drift and boost, and a garage of hand-picked cars.
  • City tracks with flyovers, tunnels and bridges, tuned for phones with URP.
  • Game rules live in a pure C# layer with no engine code, so a test suite checks every level and bots can race whole worlds headless.

04 — Web platform · AI automation

FacelessTube

A platform that runs faceless YouTube and Instagram channels end to end — from picking a topic to publishing the video.

Topic→Script→Voiceover→Stock media→Subtitles→Thumbnail→Assemble→Upload
  • Every step is a queued background job with automatic retries, so a channel keeps publishing without anyone watching it.
  • Content strategy and a content calendar per channel, with brand settings, style presets and a reusable asset library.
  • A/B tests for titles and thumbnails, YouTube analytics sync and a quality score on every video before it goes out.
  • Publishes to YouTube and Instagram, including portrait cuts for Shorts and Reels; webhooks for outside tools.

How I work

about

I like owning a product from the first sketch to the store listing: the game rules or business logic, the server, the app, the build pipeline and the release.

I keep the core logic pure and well tested — a card engine as immutable functions, a racing game's rules in plain C#, a panchang worked out from astronomy formulas — so the screens on top can change fast without breaking anything.

A lot of what I build is for Indian users — in their own languages, on ordinary Android phones.
